# Vanilla mob item pickup

Objective: vanilla hostile mobs, pets (tamed wolves and other mobs with an owner), neutral creatures (wild wolves, polar bears, iron golems...) and, optionally, mobs from other mods can pick up Risk of Rain items from the ground, gain their passive effects and show them above their heads, as the riskofrain_mobs mobs already do. Off by default.
Branch: `1.20.1-vanilla-mob-item-pickup` from `1.20.1` (`0d62155`). Delivery: ask-on-risk. Push, PR and merge are the user's.
Test runner: none in the project. Checks are `./gradlew build`, dedicated servers stopped through rcon, and the user's in-game test on Fabric and Forge.

## Specs

User request (2026-11-15): "Si se puede, que los mobs vanilla puedan juntar ror_items configurable false por defecto. Si hay alguno/s que se deba exceptuar, proponemelo". Follow-ups (2026-11-16): "los domesticables que ataquen y puedan juntar items del suelo, a menos que desaconsejes alguno. Por ejemplo lobos."; "Prefiero Cloth Config para mantener la consistencia con mobs"; "iría con la opción de no desaparecer y los iconos para mobs en esta lista"; mobs from other mods: "Una opción aparte, apagada por defecto".

- S1: Who picks up, each group behind its own setting (S7):
  - Vanilla hostile mobs (`Enemy`) except the excluded ones (S2).
  - Neutral creatures: any mob that implements `NeutralMob` and not `Enemy`, in any namespace (vanilla and other mods): wild wolves, polar bears, bees (excluded by S2), iron golems and neutral mobs from other mods. Goats, pandas, llamas, dolphins and foxes do not implement `NeutralMob` and stay out. Hoglins and zoglins implement `Enemy`, so they are already in the hostile group. The iron golem is the intended showcase: a player can hand it items. This switch governs neutral mobs everywhere, so a modded neutral mob is eligible with it even if the other-mods setting is off; the other-mods setting governs only modded `Enemy` mobs and `pickup_allowed` ones. Boss convention tags still exclude non-vanilla mobs. Not an ally group: they are creatures that are not enemies by default.
  - Pets, through their own setting only (never through the neutral, hostile or other-mods switches): any mob that implements `OwnableEntity` and has a non-null owner UUID, vanilla or from other mods (tamed wolves, and modded tamed mobs). An ownerless wolf goes through the neutral group. Items already carried stay when a wolf gets tamed. Pets that do not fight are left out through `pickup_excluded` (S2): cat, parrot, horse, donkey, mule, llama, trader llama, camel, skeleton horse and zombie horse (all the vanilla `OwnableEntity` mobs besides the wolf, verified with `javap`); foxes are not `OwnableEntity` and already carry one item in their mouth through their own logic. User (2026-11-23), replacing the original "tamed wolves" group (L14): "Dale, vamos con A".
  - Mobs from other mods (entity type namespace other than `minecraft`): those that implement `Enemy`, plus any listed in `#riskofrain_items:pickup_allowed` (data-pack editable, for modded companions or neutral mobs; there is no generic way to tell whether a mob fights for the player). Not excluded (S2), not tagged as a boss by the loader convention tag (`c:bosses` on Fabric, `forge:bosses` on Forge, existence to verify in T3), and not a mob that already has its own inventory (`InventoryCarrier`, which covers the riskofrain_mobs mobs and their own pickup).
- S2: Excluded mobs, through the entity type tag `#riskofrain_items:pickup_excluded` (data-pack editable, `"replace": false`), applied to every group: Creeper (an explosion discards the mob without drops, so the items would be lost), Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden and Elder Guardian (bosses; the Dragon also never walks over ground items), Ghast, Phantom and Vex (they fly or pass through walls and never reach ground items), Piglin (its own inventory and bartering would clash), Slime and Magma Cube (they split on death, so who keeps the items is unclear), Bee (it flies and rarely touches ground items), and the pets that do not fight: Cat, Parrot, Horse, Donkey, Mule, Llama, Trader Llama, Camel, Skeleton Horse and Zombie Horse. Shulkers are not excluded: they never move, so they only pick up what lands on them. A modded mob that misbehaves (custom death without drops, AI that skips the vanilla step) is fixed by adding it to this tag.
- S3: Pickup works as for the riskofrain_mobs mobs: only items in `#riskofrain_items:passive_items`, only while the mob's carried inventory can take the stack, stacks of the same item merge, and only while the `mobGriefing` game rule is true (as vanilla loot pickup). The ground scan runs on an interval, not every tick. It is separate from vanilla equipment pickup: a zombie still picks up armor and weapons as before, and Risk of Rain items never go to its hands or armor slots.
- S4: The carried inventory has the configured number of slots (S7), is saved and loaded with the mob, and every carried item drops when the mob dies, whatever killed it. Lowering the slot setting never deletes items a mob already carries.
- S5: Effects come from the existing holder logic (`ItemUtils.countItemStack`, `PassiveItemEffects.onHolderTick`): a carrier counts as a holder, with the same effects and exceptions as the riskofrain_mobs mobs (Infusion, Golden Gun, Smart Shopper and Soldier's Syringe do nothing on mobs; Goat Hoof and Rose Buckler trigger while moving).
- S6: With the setting on (default), a mob carrying at least one item never despawns, so its items are not lost. Balance risk: with many zombies carriers can pile up; turning the setting off lets them despawn with their items. Tamed pets never despawn anyway.
- S7: Configuration with Cloth Config (AutoConfig) and a Mod Menu entry, as in riskofrain_mobs; riskofrain_items gains the Cloth Config dependency. Nine settings and defaults:
  - Vanilla hostile mobs pick up items: false.
  - Pets pick up items (`PETS_PICK_UP`): false.
  - Mobs from other mods pick up items: false.
  - Neutral creatures pick up items: false.
  - Carried slots: 8 (1-27).
  - Carriers never despawn: true.
  - Show carried items: true.
  - Render distance: 16 blocks (1-64).
  - Display mode: when looking at the mob (as in riskofrain_mobs).
- S8: Icons above the head for every carrier of S1, with the same look and settings as the riskofrain_mobs display (including the brightness fix). The carried items are synced to the client for the icons. Drawn from a hook common to every entity, after it renders, so modded mobs with their own renderers (GeckoLib and similar) show icons too. Client-only code, nothing loads on a dedicated server. Moving the riskofrain_mobs display into riskofrain_items is backlog item 3b, not this slice, but this code is written so it can take over that display later.
- S9: The changelog gets an "Unreleased" section with the user-facing entries, as in riskofrain_mobs.
- S10: The owner of a tamed mob can take its carried items back without killing it. User request (2026-11-23): "Funcionó todo bien en Fabric también. Al probar con los lobos llegué a la conclusión de que a los mobs domesticables se les debería poder sacar los items sin tener que matarlos. Por ejemplo con crouch y right clic. Eso sería lo primero que intentaría un jugador que no sabe si eso va a funcionar". Rules: the player sneaks and right-clicks the mob with an empty main hand; only mobs with an owner (`OwnableEntity`: pets, S1) and only for the owner (owner UUID equals the player UUID); every carried item goes to the player inventory and what does not fit drops at the player's feet, with a pickup sound; the wolf does not sit or stand in that click. With nothing carried, or any condition failing, nothing happens and the normal interaction goes on. Iron golems and every ownerless mob never give items back. User: "Los golems que no devuelvan. Si un jugador le quiere dar items, tiene que considerar que si los quiere de regreso tendría que pelear con él. ... Los golems de hierro se pueden construir facilmente, si se tiene los materiales. Un lobo se debe encontrar". Exposed as `CarriedItems.takeAll(Mob, Player)` so the riskofrain_mobs drones and turrets (backlog 3b) get the same gesture.

- L12 (2026-11-23), two bugs from the in-game test, fixed in two `fix` commits. Bug 1 (S10), user: "Si solo tengo un slot disponible para recibir los items, pero el lobo tiene más de un item, me dará el primero, pero el resto desaparece. Si tengo los slots disponibles, funciona bien." The user was in CREATIVE mode; survival not tested yet. Root cause, from `javap` on `Inventory.add(int, ItemStack)` (1.20.1): when no progress is made and `player.getAbilities().instabuild` is true it does `stack.setCount(0)` and returns true (also in the free-slot branch), so `takeAll` saw "added" and never dropped the stack, the item was deleted.
